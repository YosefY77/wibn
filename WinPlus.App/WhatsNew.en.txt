# What's new, one line per change (English). Lines starting with # are skipped.
- The glass taskbars now show in screenshots (PrintScreen, Win + Shift + S and Wibn's capture)
- The bars show in streams and recordings: by themselves with OBS, Streamlabs and XSplit, or with "Show in streams" for Discord and others; their glass shows the wallpaper while streaming
- The highlight on the taskbar's app icons fades in and out smoothly
- In the taskbar's window previews, the whole picture highlights under the mouse, not just its edges
- Your custom mouse pointer can be smaller than Windows' smallest size
- New Discord notifications widget: the count, who sent each and what they said, on the desktop or an extra taskbar
- Save all of Wibn's settings to a small file and load them back, or move them to another PC (General settings)
- The "What's new" window is in English when English is chosen
