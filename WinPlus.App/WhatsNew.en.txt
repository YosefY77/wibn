# What's new, one line per change (English). Lines starting with # are skipped.
- Trim before downloading: on the Download videos page press "Trim", paste a YouTube link, pick the start and end, and only that part is downloaded
- On an extra taskbar: Discord notifications now use the Discord logo, with a red dot on it whenever there is any notification
- Fixed the glass showing the wallpaper mirrored on the extra bars and widgets
- In the desktop corner panel: right-click a program or shortcut to pin it to the taskbar
- Fixed the glass blinking on some taskbars
- The glass shows the windows behind it even with "Show in streams" on
- The info bar keeps its size instead of growing and shrinking as the numbers and text on it change
- A suggestion box on the General page: write your idea and it goes straight to the developer
- Fixed some apps pinned to Windows' taskbar (like Steam) missing from Wibn's taskbar
- Right-click any page in the side menu, or any option inside a page, to add it to "Favorites" at the top or hide it; Taskbar and Desktop are now under "Interface"
- Each group in the side menu has an arrow: click it to fold or open its pages
- Scroll the mouse wheel on the sound waves on a taskbar to change the device's volume, or on an app's icon to change just that app's volume
- New minimize animations: a Mac-like genie, shrink into the icon, fade, fall down or zoom away. Turn it on from the Animations page
