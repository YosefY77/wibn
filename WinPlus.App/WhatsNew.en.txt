# What's new, one line per change (English). Lines starting with # are skipped.
- Much less memory used with "Show in streams" on
- Fixed the mouse pointer hitching on a click with the minimize animation on
- Fixed a glass that could freeze on an old frame of the video wallpaper
- Apps you removed from Wibn's bar (like Steam games) no longer come back after the update
- The info bar goes back to its length after a long text on it is gone
- The mouse wheel on Store apps on the taskbar now changes their volume
- The suggestion box is gone; a button to support the developer is on the General page
- Switching to a video wallpaper no longer flashes a random frame of it at the start
- The minimize animation is always one of Wibn's now; Windows' usual animation option is gone
- Adding a page to favorites puts a shortcut to it at the top; it stays in its own place too
- A long title on the playback bar and the Now playing widget slides to show all of it, then starts again
- Every setting that had no icon has one now
- Wibn's window is a normal window now and no longer stays above the others
- New: "Pin on top" in any window's title-bar menu (turn it on in the General page)
- Fixed titles with both Arabic and English showing reversed on the playback bar
- New: the desktop panel groups your files into programs, games, folders and files, with an arrange button to drag the groups into the order you want
- New: right-click any item in the desktop panel → "Classify as…" to make it a game, a program or a file
- The playback bar no longer shows videos you didn't play (ones that play by themselves for a moment, or pages with a paused video)
- Wibn's hidden taskbar comes back only when the mouse is at its own spot on the edge, not anywhere along it
- The sign-in for downloading videos is now saved encrypted with your Windows account, Wibn paces its requests to protect your account, and it checks the download tools' fingerprint before running them
