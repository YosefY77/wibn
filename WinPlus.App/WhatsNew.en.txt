# What's new, one line per change (English). Lines starting with # are skipped.
- Drag Wibn's taskbar to any screen edge, with a preview of where it will land
- The clock can sit on a different edge than the apps (e.g. clock on top, apps at the bottom), and keeps its side when moved
- Change the Start button's picture: Windows logo, Wibn logo, an icon in your color, or your own picture
- Eyedropper: pick any color on the screen, with a magnifier showing its code
- The new color picker window replaces Windows' old color dialog in widgets
- Wallpapers: add your pictures and videos, press Ctrl + Alt + W and they come up in the middle of the screen, Linux style; move with the arrows and Enter changes the wallpaper with a smooth animation
- Animated wallpapers: any video becomes a wallpaper under the desktop icons, and pauses by itself while a window is maximized or a game is full screen, to save memory
- The glass on the taskbar and the extra bars shows what's behind it live (windows, videos), and all the bars change together. Glass bars don't show in streams and screenshots
- Lighter and smoother: the stutter the glass caused is fixed, and Wibn uses less memory
- New option: the bar covering the window in front slides away so the whole window shows; it comes back when the window moves away, or when you push the mouse against the screen edge over it
- In full screen: push the mouse against the top of the screen right over a bar and only the clock and the extra bar come down, without the bottom taskbar
- Simpler menus and settings: options grouped, duplicates removed
- This "What's new" window, shown after every update
