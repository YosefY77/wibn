What's new in Harmoni

Glass theme (new)
- A see-through theme: your desktop shows behind the app window
- Pick your own tint, with sliders for transparency, blur and grain
- Also applies to the bubble and the Alt+P panel

Redesigned Settings
- Split into tabs: General, Appearance, Connections, Account, Updates
- Preview of every theme before you pick it
- On/off switches instead of checkboxes

Spotify
- Fixed account linking after Spotify's latest API changes (Premium required)
- New songs and playlists you add on Spotify sync automatically on launch and every 5 minutes
- New songs appear at the top of Favorites, with a notification when something new arrives

Search
- More accurate results: originals first, fewer duplicates, remixes and short clips ranked lower
- Fixed the "HTTP Error 403" search error
- SoundCloud search is much faster

Alt+P panel
- Playlists and search added next to Favorites
- New layout for the controls, volume and seek bars
- Now shows on top of games in Borderless / Windowed Fullscreen mode, and returns you to the game when closed

Home
- Song artwork fills the whole card, and the play button is centered
- Removed the artists section
- Fixed "Recently played" and suggestions disappearing on launch

Profile
- Removed the color presets from profile editing
